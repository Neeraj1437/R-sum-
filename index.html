Helping Eyes: a wearable that reads pages aloud for visually impaired users

An ESP32-CAM on the wearable takes a photo of the page. The system finds the page in the photo, straightens and cleans it up, reads the text with a vision-language model and speaks it, with no screen and no hands needed.

  1. CaptureESP32-CAM
  2. DetectYOLOv8
  3. EnhanceOpenCV
  4. ReadQwen3-VL
  5. SpeakTTS

Key highlights

  • 98.26% character and 91.06% word accuracy on blurry ESP32-CAM photos, by passing each photo through OpenCV, YOLOv8 and Qwen3-VL
  • EasyOCR managed only 4.28% character accuracy on the same kind of photos
  • A full page goes from photo to speech in about 11 s, by chaining page detection, perspective correction, VLM text reading and text-to-speech
  • Designed the enclosure in Fusion 360 and 3D-printed it, fitting the camera, battery, charging and audio into one hands-free prototype

ReturnGuard: AI Agent that Checks Clothing Returns

A LangGraph agent that looks at a customer's photos and decides whether a clothing return holds up. I led the computer-vision side.

  • Fine-tuned two YOLO11 models as the agent's tools: one says if clothes are damaged, the other marks where
  • Damage evidence at 90.7% accuracy, 96.4% recall; 81.9% on 199 real Amazon photos
  • Caught all 6 wrong-item photos in a test on real Amazon orders
  • Compares CLIP image embeddings of the customer's photo with the catalog photos, plus a Gemini check of the item's type and colour

A roadside unit runs a small vision-language model and gives drivers spoken warnings about the road ahead.
