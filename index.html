ReturnGuard: AI agent that checks clothing returns

A LangGraph agent that looks at a customer's photos and decides whether a clothing return holds up (team of 4; I led computer vision). Two fine-tuned YOLO11 models find damage at 90.7% accuracy, 96.4% recall, and the agent caught all 6 wrong-item photos in a test on real Amazon orders. It also compares CLIP embeddings of the customer's photo with the catalog photos, plus a Gemini check of the item's type and colour.
